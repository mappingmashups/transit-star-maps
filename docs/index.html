★Transit Star Maps★

Subway and light rail networks, drawn as constellations.

Every star is a station, placed where it really is, and lines join neighboring stations the way star charts draw constellations. Bigger stars mark the places where lines meet or branch. The result is a city's transit system as it might appear in the night sky.
